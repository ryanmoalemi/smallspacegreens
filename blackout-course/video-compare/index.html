DAY 03: BLACKOUT LIGHTING & ALERTS
BUILD YOUR NIGHTTIME BACKUP

By the end of this mission, your home has working lights you can reach, a backup phone charge, and a way to receive local warnings.

Tap any numbered photo to preview that step's instructional walkthrough.

LIGHT THE BEDSIDE

Place a working flashlight within easy reach of every occupied bed. Keep shoes close and the path clear.

LIGHT THE WAY OUT

Set a separate, battery-powered lantern in a safe shared location. Test it without blocking doors or stairs.

CHARGE YOUR BACKUPS

Charge a power bank and put the correct phone cable beside it. Test that your phone actually receives power.

TEST YOUR ALERTS

Confirm your weather radio is working and phone emergency alerts are enabled. Check your local alert-signup options.

AT A
GLANCE

GEAR
Flashlights, lantern, correct batteries, power bank, charging cable, weather radio if available
TASK TIME
15–30 minutes after supplies are ready
SUPPLIES COST
Depends on what you already own

SAFETY
NOTE

Use battery-powered lighting rather than candles. A regular plug-in nightlight will not work during an outage unless it has battery backup. Keep exits clear.
