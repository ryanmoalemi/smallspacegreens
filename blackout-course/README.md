## Approved design and audience principles

Owner-selected visual system:
1. Olive textured headline: DAY NN: ...
2. Orange action-led banner: FIND & LABEL / GATHER & SECURE / ...
3. Mustard brand ribbon: 30 DAY BLACKOUT PLAN
4. Four clearly numbered turquoise photo-video controls, dark navy instructional typography, parchment “AT A GLANCE” sidebar.
5. No title icon, no sources printed on each mission; source references collected separately. Completion badges are website-only.

For older learners, use big obvious Play/Pause buttons, high-contrast persistent captions, predictable next/previous navigation and no autoplay. Preserve the corresponding **real verified photo** through subtle motion; do not invent physical equipment positions. Use a natural, consistent adult male narrator. Future production can encode one 3–5-minute master per day and four step-chapter entry points, to be tested against separate four-clip navigation.
