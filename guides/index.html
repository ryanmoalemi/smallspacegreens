Growing guides

Pick the job in front of you. The full method, the sources, and the next step are on each page.

Growing steps come from university extension publications. This site has not run its own grow trials. The seed calculator turns a published 10 by 20 inch rate into grams for your tray.

Microgreens

  • How to grow microgreens in an apartment: your first tray

    Pick an edible crop, size the tray, and follow one published method. A lamp is optional until the window falls short.

  • Can you grow microgreens on a windowsill?

    Measure the sill, watch the stems, and add light only if the plants lean or stay pale.

  • Microgreens seed density by crop and tray size

    Start with the crop and the tray. The calculator applies a published rate only when that rate is crop-specific.

  • Pea shoots vs radish microgreens: which should you grow?

    Radish is spicy and lighter on the scale. Pea is milder, soaked, and a much heavier sowing.

  • How to bottom water microgreens and seed trays

    Wet the mix from below, then empty the reservoir. A moldy crop is discarded, not rescued by a lighter pour.

Seed starting

  • Starting vegetable seeds in an apartment

    These plants are transplants. They need more light and more weeks than a tray of microgreens.

Balcony growing

  • Plan a balcony salad garden

    Match the crop to the hours of sun. Smaller pots are easier to move. They do not prove the balcony can hold the weight.
