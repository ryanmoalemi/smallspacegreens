Grow a 3 hour salad on your balcony

3 hours = 3 hours of direct sun a day.

The 3 hour layout

Start with this plan if you measured about 3 hours of direct sun. Two 8 inch pots hold one lettuce each, and a small herb pot sits beside them. If you later measure about 4 hours or more, the balcony salad garden guide has a second layout that adds a 10 inch pot of radishes or scallions. Fruiting crops still need about 6 to 10 hours.

Pots

Use two 8 inch pots with one lettuce each, plus a small herb pot. Check pot diameter and depth before you order. UC Marin wants at least about 6 inches of depth. Every pot needs a hole. Empty the saucer. Smaller pots are easier to move, but that does not establish how much weight the balcony can support. Secure pots so a gust cannot send them off a railing.

Soil

Use container mix, not ground soil. The handout says ground soil is too heavy. Do not put rocks in the bottom. A hole is what lets water leave. If any topsoil is added, keep it to about 10 to 15 percent. Make a supply list for this layout: the selected crops, pots with drainage holes and saucers, and container mix.

What to plant

Leafy greens can do well with as little as about 3 hours of direct sun. Plant one lettuce in each 8 inch pot, and use the small pot for an herb. Do not add radishes or scallions on that sun budget. If the balcony fails the sun test, do not force a tomato. Grow leaves indoors as microgreens.

Watering

Plan where you will stand to water, and where drained water will go. Every pot needs a hole so extra water can leave. Empty the saucer. Do not put rocks in the bottom of the pot. Check allowable balcony load before you add wet mix and stored water.

Harvest

Wash what you cut. The FDA says to rinse produce under running water, including food you grew, and not to use soap.

FAQ

How much sun does a salad need?

The UC ANR handout says leafy crops can do well with as little as about 3 hours, roots about 4 hours, and fruiting crops about 6 to 10 hours. Measure your balcony.

What pot should I start with?

An 8 inch pot for one lettuce, plus a small herb pot, if you have about 3 hours of sun. All of them need a drainage hole. Check diameter and depth before ordering. UC Marin wants at least about 6 inches of depth.

Can I use soil from the ground?

No. The handout says ground soil is too heavy. Use a container mix. Do not put rocks in the bottom.

Can I add radishes to a 3 hour balcony?

No. Do not add radishes to a 3 hour plan. Roots such as radishes want about 4 hours. The full sun layouts add a 10 inch pot of radishes or scallions only when you have about 4 hours or more.
