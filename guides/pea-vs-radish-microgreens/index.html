Pea shoots vs radish microgreens: which should you grow?

Radish is spicy and lighter on the scale. Pea is milder, soaked, and a much heavier sowing.

By Ryan Moalemi. Updated November 10, 2026.

We earn a commission from purchases through this page. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Choose radish for a spicy green, no presoak in the Utah State table, and 42 grams on a 10 by 20 inch tray. Choose pea for a milder shoot, a soak, and 150 grams on that same tray. Pea does not automatically cost more. Your price and the grams decide.

Pea

Seed per 10 by 20 in (Utah State)150 g

PresoakAbout 6 hours (Utah State). Maryland lists 6 to 12 hours.

Cover (Utah State)About 3 days, plus weight so seed stays down

Harvest (Utah State)About 8 to 10 days

Flavor (Utah State)Sweet pea

Weigh pea dry, then soak. A half-size 10 by 10 inch tray is about 21 grams of radish or 75 grams of pea if you scale by area. Do not mix them in one tray. They do not finish together. Radish skips the presoak in this method, which removes one preparation step for your first tray. Discard a moldy crop. Do not try to dry it out and eat it.

FAQ

Which is faster?

Utah State lists pea at about 8 to 10 days and radish at about 8 to 12, with a longer cover for pea. Mississippi State puts both in a 7 to 10 day group. Check the tray.

Do I soak radish?

Utah State lists no presoak for radish. Pea is a soak of about 6 hours in the Utah State method.

Can they share a tray?

No. Use two smaller trays. Extension guides say not to mix species that finish on different days.

Which costs more?

Multiply each crop's dry seed weight by its price per gram. Pea uses more grams in the Utah State method, but a lower price per gram can offset that. Use your actual bag prices in the calculator to compare the same tray size.

Evidence notes

Illinois lists radish with faster crops and pea with slower germination. Di Gioia recommends weight on pea during germination. Utah State's general method uses about 2 to 5 pounds on the cover for part of the dark period on crops it weights. Flavor words above are Utah State's table, not a tasting on this site. Mississippi State's radish example is about 2 to 3 tablespoons per 10 by 20 inch tray, a volume, not 42 grams.
