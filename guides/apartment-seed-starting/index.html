Starting vegetable seeds in an apartment

These plants are transplants. They need more light and more weeks than a tray of microgreens.

By Ryan Moalemi. Updated November 10, 2026.

We earn a commission from purchases through this page. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Name the crop and where the mature plant will live. Then count back from that outdoor date. Tomatoes, peppers, and eggplants are transplants, not food at the seedling stage. Illinois Extension says not to grow them as microgreens because of toxins in the stems and foliage.

Plan your sowing date, then prepare the pots, mix, and light.

Those weeks are relative to your outdoor date. A hardiness zone is not a calendar. Slide the dates for your last frost. Reiners's Cornell table assumes a May 20 frost as an example. The UMass planting chart is for Massachusetts.

Light, mix, and steps

  1. 1
    Use a sterile soilless mix and pots with holes, about 3 inches deep in Brewer's list. Do not use garden soil.
  2. 2
    Give light close to the leaves for a long day. Reiners describes fluorescents about 4 inches above the plants for 16 to 18 hours. Brewer says about 3 to 6 inches for 12 to 16 hours. A lamp across the room is not that advice.
  3. 3
    Sow at the packet depth, about 2 to 3 times the seed width. Keep sown pots warm until they emerge, then move them under the lamp.
  4. 4
    Water from below for no more than about an hour, then drain. See bottom watering.
  5. 5
    Thin at two true leaves. Feed at half strength then, not on the day you sow. Harden off for about one to two weeks before the balcony.
  6. 6
    If you reuse cups, follow a sanitizer labeled for that surface and the cited handout. Do not invent a bleach strength, and do not mix cleaning products. Rinse when the label says to rinse.

If the plants will move to a balcony, check space, sunlight, and containers for the mature crop before starting more seedlings than you can grow there.

Keep seedlings off the microgreen plate

Keep transplant seedlings separate from the microgreens you plan to eat. Do not harvest tomato, pepper, or eggplant seedlings as microgreens. Choose an identified edible microgreen crop and follow the apartment microgreens guide. Sow fewer transplants than the shelf can light. Plants started too early outgrow the indoor spot.

FAQ

Can I start tomatoes on a windowsill with no lamp?

Cornell sources say no if you want sturdy transplants. A window is a better fit for a short microgreen crop.

How early should I sow?

About six weeks for tomatoes, peppers, and eggplants, and about three weeks for cucumbers, melons, and squash, counted back from your outdoor date.

What mix should I use?

A sterile soilless seed-starting mix. Cornell and the Sonoma County handout both say to skip garden soil.

When do I fertilize?

After a second set of true leaves, at half strength. Microgreen trays are different: Maryland says they do not need fertilizer because you harvest so soon.
