First-tray checklist

Print this list and fill it in, or have Ryan email you a copy after you confirm your address.

The steps follow the apartment method on your first tray, which cites University of Maryland Extension, Utah State University Extension, and Penn State Extension. This list is a reminder, not a new rate.

  • Date sown: ________
  • Crop (not tomato, pepper, or eggplant seedlings): ________
  • Tray inside length and width: ________
  • Seed grams from the calculator, dry weight: ________
  • Presoak, if the crop needs it: ________
  • Mix or mat, about 1 to 1.5 inches if you use mix: ________
  • Drained tray nested in a solid tray: yes / no
  • Covered on: ________ Uncovered on: ________
  • Light: window only, or lamp. Hours: ________
  • Stems leaning or pale? Add light rather than sowing the same tray again.
  • Mold on the crop: discard it and clean the trays. Do not eat it.
  • Harvest date: ________ Cut above the mix. Wash before eating.

If you want the longer explanation of any line, use the first-tray guide. A lamp, a fan, and a bought kit are optional. A clean drained container you already own can be the tray.
