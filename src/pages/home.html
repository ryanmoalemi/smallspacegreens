First-tray plan

  1. Choose an edible crop. Radish and pea are the usual pair to compare. Do not eat tomato, pepper, or eggplant seedlings as microgreens. Compare pea and radish.
  2. Size the tray and the seed. A 10 by 20 inch flat is the reference. Smaller trays use less seed. Calculate the grams.
  3. Follow the growing method. Soilless mix, a drained tray, bottom water, and light after germination. Read the first-tray steps.

Kit or DIY

A kit bundles a small amount of seed with a tray. A DIY setup buys trays you can wash and seed in a larger bag. The first purchase can look similar. Later trays usually cost less when the seed is bought in bulk, which is the point University of Maryland Extension makes about tiny packets.

This site does not list live shop links or current retail prices yet. The comparison explains what to count, and it shows a dated extension price snapshot next to a recalculation so the arithmetic is visible.
