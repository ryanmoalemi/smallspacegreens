Small-space gardening, explained plainly

Small Space Greens is a set of practical how-to guides for people growing microgreens and salad greens without a yard. The pages are written from university extension publications, with the sources linked, so you can check the method yourself.

How these pages are written

Ryan Moalemi edits the site. The guides do not report grow tests from this site, because none have been run. Where two extension publications give different seed rates or storage times, the page says so instead of smoothing them into one number.

There are no paid placements. Affiliate links are not live yet. If a product link is added later, the page will say so at the top and the affiliate disclosure explains how a commission would work. Questions and corrections go to ryan@smallspacegreens.com.
