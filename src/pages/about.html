About Small Space Greens

Practical setups for a windowsill, counter, shelf, or small balcony. The focus is microgreens first, then the seed starting and balcony steps that sit next to them.

Published by Ryan Moalemi. Updated November 9, 2026.

Who runs it

Ryan Moalemi writes and publishes this site. The contact address is ryan@smallspacegreens.com. The aim is to make growing in a small home easier to plan, with advice you can check and tools you can use.

The problem the site is for: people who want edible greens without a yard, and who need a tray size, a seed weight, and a watering step they can check against an extension publication.

How recommendations are chosen

Growing steps come from university extension publications and a small number of other public agency pages, linked at the end of each guide. When two of those sources disagree, the page says so beside the step, then still gives one action to take.

This site has not run grow trials. When a firsthand test exists, the page will say what was measured, how many times, and what was not measured. Photo credits link to the original sources, licenses, and crop details. Diagrams explain a method or measurement; they are not photographs of a finished crop.

The gear page lists categories only: a tray pair, mix, a lamp, a mister, scissors, and a fan. It does not name brands or link to stores. There are no live affiliate links and no store links. The rules are on the editorial method page. As of November 9, 2026, that is still true.

Corrections

If a sentence misreads a source, or a step is unclear, write to ryan@smallspacegreens.com with the page address. Ryan Moalemi is responsible for corrections and product research.
