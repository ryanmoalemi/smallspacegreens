How product links work

A clear explanation of recommendations and commissions.

Updated November 9, 2026.

Current status: There are no live affiliate links and no store links. The gear page lists categories only. No affiliate program is connected.

Our recommendations

Growing steps come from published extension sources. A future product note would name the evidence, a limitation, and the date it was checked. Read the editorial method for the details.

A company cannot pay for a placement or a more favorable recommendation. You can use every guide and the calculator without a product link, because none are on the site.

If affiliate links are added

An affiliate link may earn Small Space Greens a commission when a reader makes a qualifying purchase. The seller or network pays that commission under its program. Before any such links are used, the relevant page will clearly explain the relationship near its recommendations and this notice will be updated.

If a store link is added later, the purchase would take place on the merchant's website. Confirm the selected variation, current price, delivery costs, returns, and availability there. A product's presence in a guide would not guarantee that it is in stock.

Cookies and questions

Merchants have their own privacy and cookie policies. Future affiliate links may use tracking to attribute a purchase. Our privacy policy explains the services currently used by this site.
