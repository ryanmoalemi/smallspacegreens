How product links work

A clear explanation of recommendations and commissions.

Current status: Some guides link to LetPot countertop gardens and to True Leaf Market seed and kit collections through Awin, and to other supplies through Amazon Associates. Amazon Associates links

We earn a commission if you buy through these links, at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Some guides, the setup page, and the seed calculator link to a specific product. The button on those lists says Check price on Amazon. The gift guide uses photo cards, and its button says View on Amazon. Those card photos are example photos, not Amazon's photos. Microgreens seed links go to True Leaf Market, and LetPot gardens go to LetPot, instead of Amazon. Confirm the variant, current price, delivery, returns, and whether it is in stock on the seller's page. A link does not mean this site has used that product. Prices are not copied here. There is no coupon code.

Our recommendations

Growing steps come from published extension sources. A LetPot note repeats what LetPot's own page says. A True Leaf Market note describes the collection, not a growing result. Read the editorial method for the details.

A company cannot pay for a more favorable recommendation. You can use every guide and the calculator without clicking a product link.

Cookies and questions

LetPot, True Leaf Market, Awin, and Amazon have their own privacy and cookie policies. An affiliate link can use tracking to attribute a purchase. Our privacy policy explains the services this site uses.
