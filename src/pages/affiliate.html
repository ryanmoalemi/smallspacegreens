How product links work

A clear explanation of recommendations and commissions.

Updated November 9, 2026.

Current status: The merchant links in our researched starter comparison are ordinary links. Small Space Greens does not earn a commission from them. No affiliate program is currently connected.

Our recommendations

We compare products against a practical growing need, using clearly identified evidence. Manufacturer specifications, our editorial assessment, and any future firsthand test are separate evidence categories. Read the editorial method for the details.

A company cannot pay for a placement or a more favorable recommendation. You can use every guide and the calculator without clicking a product link.

If affiliate links are added

An affiliate link may earn Small Space Greens a commission when a reader makes a qualifying purchase. The seller or network pays that commission under its program. Before any such links are used, the relevant page will clearly explain the relationship near its recommendations and this notice will be updated.

Purchases take place on the merchant's website. Confirm the selected variation, current price, delivery costs, returns, and availability there. A product's presence in a guide does not guarantee that it is in stock.

Cookies and questions

Merchants have their own privacy and cookie policies. Future affiliate links may use tracking to attribute a purchase. Our privacy policy explains the services currently used by this site.
