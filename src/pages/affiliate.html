Affiliate disclosure

Plain language on commissions, prices, and how recommendations are chosen.

Some pages on Small Space Greens may later include affiliate links. If you buy through one of those links, we may earn a commission from a qualifying purchase. The price you pay is not increased because of that commission. The seller pays the commission under its affiliate program.

Recommendations are independent. A guide is written from published sources, mainly university extension services, and from the practical question the page is trying to answer. A company cannot pay for a mention, a ranking, or a kinder sentence. If a product link exists only because it pays a commission, it does not belong in the guide.

As of November 9, 2026, there are no live affiliate links on the site. Supply lists leave a marked spot in the page code for a future link, and that spot is not a visible link you can click. When a real link is added, the page will keep a short disclosure near the top, and this page will still be linked from it.

Affiliate programs may use cookies or similar tools, set after you click through, to know that a purchase followed a link from this site. That is described in the privacy policy. You do not need to click a product link to use any guide or the calculator.

If a sentence and a link ever seem to disagree, trust the sentence that cites a source, and write to ryan@smallspacegreens.com so it can be fixed. This notice is meant to meet the spirit of the Federal Trade Commission Endorsement Guides: a clear statement, easy to see, in ordinary language, close to the pages that may carry a link.
