Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026. This policy covers Small Space Greens at smallspacegreens.com.

Small Space Greens is published by Ryan Moalemi. The contact email is ryan@smallspacegreens.com.

What the site is

The site is a collection of static pages and one calculator that runs in your browser. You do not create an account. The calculator does not send tray sizes, crops, or prices to a server. Those numbers stay on your device for that visit.

Analytics

As of November 2026, the pages do not load a third-party analytics script. There is no analytics dashboard operated by this site, and no analytics cookie is set by these pages.

If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before or at the same time. A typical analytics tool records a page view, a rough location derived from an IP address, the browser type, and which link was used to arrive. That kind of tool often sets a cookie so it can tell a new visit from a return visit. You would be able to read that change here, and the script would be named.

The host, GitHub Pages, may keep its own connection logs, which can include an IP address, the page requested, and the time. Those logs are controlled by the host, not by a script in these pages. This site does not use them to build a profile of readers.

Cookies

The pages themselves do not set tracking cookies or advertising cookies. A cookie is a small text file a site asks your browser to store. Essential cookies, if a host sets any to deliver the page, are part of serving the file. They are not used here to follow you across other websites.

Affiliate tracking cookies

The site does not currently include affiliate links. If it does later, a click on an affiliate link can take you to another website. That other website, or the affiliate network it uses, may set a cookie or similar identifier so a purchase can be attributed to the link. That cookie is set by the other site after you leave, not by the guide while you are reading it.

An affiliate cookie is used to tell the program that a visit came from this site, which is how a commission would be calculated. It is not required to read the guides. You can avoid it by not clicking an affiliate link. The affiliate disclosure explains the commission in plain language. Prices are not raised to cover a commission.

Email

If you email ryan@smallspacegreens.com, the message is read so it can be answered. The address, the content, and any attachments you include are stored in the email account that receives them. They are not added to a mailing list. Do not send sensitive documents you do not want kept in email. You can ask for a message to be deleted by replying to the thread.

Children

The site is written for adults planning a garden. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. The November 2026 version matches a site with no analytics script and no live affiliate links.
