Privacy policy

Updated November 9, 2026. This policy covers Small Space Greens at smallspacegreens.com.

Small Space Greens is published by Ryan Moalemi. The contact email is ryan@smallspacegreens.com.

What the site is

The site is a collection of static pages, one calculator that runs in your browser, and an optional email signup for the first-tray checklist. You do not create an account. The calculator does not send tray sizes, crops, or prices to a server. Those numbers stay on your device for that visit.

Analytics

The pages load Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-8YDK0NYXM5, using the standard gtag.js snippet. Google Analytics uses cookies. It records page views, a rough location derived from your IP address, browser and device information, and how you reached the page. It also records three events from this site: a checklist signup after the form succeeds, a calculator result when you press Calculate, and a video play when you start an embedded clip. Those events do not include your email address or the seed price you typed.

Google processes this analytics data for the site. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. To opt out in your browser, you can install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

The host, GitHub Pages, may keep its own connection logs, which can include an IP address, the page requested, and the time. Those logs are controlled by the host. This site does not use them to build a profile of readers.

Embedded videos

Some guides include a YouTube video behind a play button. The player is not loaded until you press play. It then comes from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. When the video plays, YouTube may set cookies and receive your IP address, browser details, and which video you started. That data is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. You can read the guide without playing a video. The caption names the channel. These clips were not filmed for Small Space Greens.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies so it can tell visits apart and remember that you have been here. A cookie is a small text file a site asks your browser to store. The pages do not set a separate advertising cookie. You can block or delete cookies in your browser. The checklist and the guides still work if you do.

Affiliate tracking cookies

The site does not currently include affiliate links. If it does later, a click on an affiliate link can take you to another website. That other website, or the affiliate network it uses, may set a cookie or similar identifier so a purchase can be attributed to the link. That cookie is set by the other site after you leave, not by the guide while you are reading it.

An affiliate cookie would be used to tell the program that a visit came from this site, which is how a commission would be calculated. It is not required to read the guides. The affiliate disclosure explains the commission in plain language. Prices are not raised to cover a commission.

Checklist email

If you use the checklist form, the site sends your email address to a signup function hosted by Supabase. The form also sends a website field that people are not asked to fill in. That field is there to catch automated signups. Supabase processes the request. The address is stored with Resend, the email provider, so Ryan Moalemi can send you the first-tray checklist.

The signup is double opt-in. You get a message first and must confirm your address before the checklist is sent. You can unsubscribe with the link in any email, or by writing to ryan@smallspacegreens.com. The printable checklist on the site does not require an email address.

Other email

If you email ryan@smallspacegreens.com directly, the message is read so it can be answered. The address, the content, and any attachments you include are stored in that email account. They are not added to the checklist list unless you used the signup form and confirmed. Do not send sensitive documents you do not want kept in email. You can ask for a message to be deleted by replying to the thread.

Children

The site is written for adults planning a garden. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes
