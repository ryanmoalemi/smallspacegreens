Contact

Write to Ryan Moalemi about a broken link, a confusing step, a product that is no longer available, or a setup question.

Email ryan@smallspacegreens.com.

Include the page address and enough detail to find the problem: the sentence, the crop, the tray size, or the link that failed. A photo of a tray can help explain a question. It is not, by itself, a reason to change a published seed rate.

Grow reports are welcome as leads. They are not treated as a controlled comparison. A change to a cited number needs a published source, or a method written down before the result, which this site does not have yet.

Please do not send passwords, payment card numbers, or anything else you would not want stored in email. The privacy policy explains how mail is handled. There is no account and no contact form, because these are static pages.

Questions about future product links belong with the affiliate disclosure.
