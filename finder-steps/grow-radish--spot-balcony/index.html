Fit, daily care & your first 3 steps

Measure first: Each pot is 8 × 8 × 6.7 in. Allow extra width for saucers and leaves; two pots need more than a 16 × 8 in strip. Herbs may need larger pots as they mature.

Timing: For small spring radishes in suitable conditions. Low window light can slow root development.

Care: Keep moisture even and give each root its own space.

  1. Sow directly in the final pot, about ½ inch deep.
  2. Thin seedlings to about 2 inches apart and water evenly.
  3. Check roots at harvest size and pull promptly; sow a fresh batch for more.
