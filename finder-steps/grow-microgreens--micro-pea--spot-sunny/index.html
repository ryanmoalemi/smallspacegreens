Fit, daily care & your first 3 steps

Measure first: Use one tray at a time. Leave open space above for shoots and access for watering. A dim spot also needs the separate standing light.

Timing: Research estimates are 8–10 days in controlled conditions. Variety and home conditions can take longer.

Care: Check moisture daily; drain away excess water.

  1. Soak seed for about 6 hours, then sow in clean, moist medium; follow the packet if it differs.
  2. Cover during germination, then uncover and provide bright light.
  3. Cut shoots above the medium with clean scissors and wash before eating.
