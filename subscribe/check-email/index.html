Check your email

Confirm your address before the checklist is sent.

Look for a message from Small Space Greens. Open it and confirm your address. The first-tray checklist is sent after that confirmation.

If it is not in your inbox after a few minutes, check the spam folder. You can also read the same list now on the checklist page.

To stop mail later, use the unsubscribe link in any message, or email ryan@smallspacegreens.com.
