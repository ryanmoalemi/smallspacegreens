About Small Space Greens

Practical setups for a windowsill, counter, shelf, or small balcony. The focus is microgreens first, then the seed starting and balcony steps that sit next to them.

Published by Ryan Moalemi. Updated November 9, 2026.

Who runs it

Ryan Moalemi writes and publishes this site. The contact address is ryan@smallspacegreens.com. There is no separate editorial board and no claimed university appointment. The useful part is the method, cited in public, not a biography.

The problem the site is for: people who want edible greens without a yard, and who need a tray size, a seed weight, and a watering step they can check against an extension publication.

How recommendations are chosen

Growing steps come from university extension publications and a small number of other public agency pages, linked at the end of each guide. When two of those sources disagree, the page says so beside the step, then still gives one action to take.

This site has not run grow trials. When a firsthand test exists, the page will say what was measured, how many times, and what was not measured. Until then, do not read a photo or a gram rate as our result. Photos are credited to their authors. Diagrams are drawings of the method, not pictures of a finished crop from this site.

Product names will appear only when a specific item has been checked for the job it claims to do. A commission will not be the reason it is listed. The rules are on the editorial method page. As of November 9, 2026, there are no live affiliate links.

Corrections

If a sentence misreads a source, or a step is unclear, write to ryan@smallspacegreens.com with the page address. Ryan Moalemi is the person who owns corrections and, later, product checks.
