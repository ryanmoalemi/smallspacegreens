# Monetization for three fortune-telling demos
Prepared 2026-11-22. All prices below are proposed tests, and all conversion assumptions are hypothetical. No partner acceptance or payment integration exists yet.

## 1. Personal Destiny Blueprint
- Entry: free birthday-based profile with a few original reflection prompts, then a transparent preview of a deeper report.
- First paid offer to test: $19 one-time expanded personal report. Test $29 only if materially richer annual-theme exercises are delivered. Avoid a subscription until repeat-use behavior exists.
- Affiliate layer: relevant journal or oracle deck after the useful reading; never gate basic results behind product purchase.
- Suggested implementation: clearly marked free premium preview with an actual printable/downloadable report in demo. Once ready, a hosted checkout and webhook should unlock purchased file fulfillment. Stripe is a possible US checkout candidate, subject to actual account/category eligibility, not a confirmed integration.
- Audience/creative hypothesis: curious adults seeking language for strengths and a new chapter. Test concrete reflective prompts instead of guaranteed predictions.
- Initial priority: best candidate for direct monetization because revenue comes from our product rather than small retail commissions. This is strategic reasoning, not tested demand.

## 2. Cosmic Chemistry
- Entry: two birth dates and a few chosen preferences yield an entertainment comparison and useful conversation questions. Do not assign a fabricated scientific success probability.
- Paid test: $19 or $29 one-time couple report/gift printable, with clearly specified deliverables and no automatic renewal.
- Affiliate: Get Closer Couples cards, then optional Keen referral when approved. Keen main page confirms a bounty but does not publish an amount. Its FAQ says $50; conflicting platform/payout references mean the live agreed offer must be confirmed before budgeting around that number.
- Strong candidate channel: shareable results and partner invitations, with names/date data kept out of public share URLs by default.
- Leading metric: share-to-new-visitor rate, then approved revenue per visitor.

## 3. Starseed Ritual Finder
- Entry: a short symbolic archetype quiz plus practical taste/activity preferences. Match an original 7-day ritual and 1-3 exact products.
- Affiliate: Art of Tea (10% publicly listed, 30-day cookie via AWIN) and Intelligent Change (10% on its direct approved creator program). Hay House ended its external program; decks can become Amazon product links once a suitable approved tracking setup is ready.
- Paid test: $9 original 7-day ritual workbook; optional $19 bundle only after demonstrating added value. A future branded tea/product line is a separate margin and inventory decision.
- Match logic: floral vs mint plus ingredient avoidance and tea vs cards vs journaling. If ingredients are avoided, suggest the deck/journal rather than force a tea match.
- Initial traffic: organic community content and permission-based email before scaled paid acquisition. Low-priced retail commissions usually leave little room for click costs; actual funnel data must establish the budget.

## Concrete unit economics, illustrative only
Use net revenue after actual refunds/commission reversals, not clicks or quoted headline rates.

Direct product revenue per visitor = result completion rate × paid purchase rate among completers × selling price.
Affiliate revenue per visitor = result completion rate × merchant click rate × merchant purchase rate × commissionable order value × commission rate.
Contribution per visitor = revenue per visitor minus payment fees, fulfillment costs, refund/reversal allowance, support, and ad cost per visitor.
Maximum break-even CPC equals contribution per visitor before ads, not order price.

Example A, own $19 report:
1,000 visitors × 65% completion × 3% paid conversion among completers = 19.5 expected orders.
Gross revenue = $370.50. At Stripe's publicly listed US domestic-card baseline of 2.9% + $0.30, fee arithmetic is about $16.59 (per-transaction rounding may slightly differ), leaving about $353.91 before refunds, delivery, support, tax and advertising, or $0.354 per visitor. Those completion/conversion assumptions have not been tested. Do not mistake $0.354 for profit or a safe ad bid.

Example B, products only:
1,000 visitors × 65% completion × 30% merchant click rate × 4% merchant conversion × $45 commissionable basket × 10% commission = $35.10, or $0.035 per visitor before reversals and traffic costs.
This illustrates why a small retail commission alone can be difficult to fund with paid social. It is not a forecast.
