SMALL SPACE GREENS: PHOTO LIST DESIGN INSTRUCTIONS
Updated November 9, 2026

Use a relevant real photograph for every editorial list item. This applies to supplies, growing steps, daily checks, setup choices, shopping checklists, related guides and printable checklist rows. Crop comparisons should show recognizable crop photographs too.

Use photographs of the actual item or activity whenever available. A tray photo may identify tray equipment, but do not describe it as a photograph of washing, covering or bottom watering when those actions are not shown. Keep alt text literal. Do not present reference photographs as our own product tests or growing trials.

Use licensed real photographs only. Do not use AI-generated plants, equipment, process photos or invented products. Do not copy a Reddit, X or blog photo on the assumption that a credit grants reuse permission.

Keep the visible caption simple: Credit: Photographer. Link the name to the matching record on /photo-credits/. Keep source, license and crop details in that central record rather than repeating them beside every thumbnail.

Keep lists compact. Standard mobile rows use an 84-pixel thumbnail column, a 15-pixel gap and readable text beside the photo. Narrow screens reduce the thumbnail to 72 pixels. Supply checklists and print layouts use smaller images. Avoid tall empty cards, giant arrows and oversized number badges.

Use src/list-photos.json as the shared thumbnail registry. Each key records thumbnail, alt, credit_id and author. Keep optimized 320 by 320 JPEG thumbnails in assets/photos/thumbs/. Full reference images are in assets/photos/.

For a source list item, add data-photo="KEY" to the li element. The builder generates the thumbnail and credit while preserving the item's content. For a crop or equipment table row, add data-photo="KEY" to tr. For a standalone photo in a comparison component, insert the PHOTO:KEY comment token using the existing source examples.

Keep credit links outside checkbox labels and outside guide-card links. Preserve checkbox data-supply values, radio setup-route values, form IDs and existing calculator behavior when editing the design.

Build using python3 scripts/build_site.py. The build rejects editorial list items without photographs, unresolved photo tokens, missing thumbnail files and missing central credit records. The November 9 photo update covers 111 editorial list-item occurrences across the 23 generated pages.

Before publishing, inspect the first-tray supplies, growing steps, setup choices, shopping checklist, crop comparisons and related guides at 320 and 390 pixels, plus a desktop layout. Check for horizontal overflow, illegible credits, incorrect crops and broken image loading. Check that the supply list still counts and copies only unchecked essentials.

Future product recommendations should use accurate photographs of the named product and variant, with approved merchant links and clear decision-making information. Do not substitute generic reference photos for a product being recommended.
