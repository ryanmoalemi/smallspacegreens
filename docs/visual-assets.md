## Presentation and reuse

Use a single short caption below each photograph: `Credit: <a href="/photo-credits/#ASSET-STEM">Photographer</a>`. The anchor ID is the exact local image filename without `.jpg`. Keep this credit link outside guide-card links and readable on phones. Do not add source, license, cropping or reference-photo text to the image caption.

Maintain the complete attribution at `/photo-credits/#ASSET-STEM`: original title, creator and profile, original source, license, matching thumbnail, and crop or resize notes. Preserve the original license for thumbnails and social crops; cropped Teogenesis and Netha Hussain photographs remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. The Photo credits page includes every published photograph, including compact list thumbnails and the lettuce photograph retained in social imagery. Do not present these photographs as evidence of a trial conducted by this site.

The eight oversized step illustrations and homepage space-selector drawings have been removed from the rendered site. Growing steps now use compact photographic rows, with real equipment or action thumbnails and short credits. Supply lists, crop choices and other editorial lists use relevant real photos through the shared `src/list-photos.json` registry. Guide-directory thumbnails use credited photographs. Decorative window, kit-versus-DIY and balcony-layout drawings are not in the repository. The seed-density bar chart remains, because it is a data chart of published gram rates. The two-tray watering drawing has been replaced by the KVDP photograph below, with the water and drain steps kept as text. The nominal tray-area boxes were removed. No licensed photograph showed a 10 by 20 inch tray, a 10 by 10 inch tray, and a 5 by 5 inch tray together at one scale, so that comparison stays as text on the seed-density guide and the calculator. Unused drawing files for harvest-window bars, icon symbols, and the photo-soon placeholder are not in the repository.

## Photo-backed list assets

The ten photographs below were added for equipment, sowing, watering, lighting and harvest rows. Each full asset has a longest edge of at most 960 pixels. All list thumbnails are 320 × 320 optimized progressive JPEGs in `assets/photos/thumbs/`. The eight existing photographs also have dedicated thumbnails; their full-size files are unchanged. Existing sunflower and equipment images are framed when needed to retain the complete subject. Other thumbnails use relevant square crops. No AI generation, color replacement or invented objects were used.

The registry maps semantic content keys to a thumbnail filename, a literal image description, a credit anchor and a photographer name. `basil-window` means mature basil in the mosaic pot; `basil` means young seedlings. `radish` uses verified radish seeds. `broccoli` uses the photograph of both broccoli and radish trays, with alt text identifying both crops rather than guessing which tray is which. `cover` uses a clear seed-tray dome photograph of its own, so it never collides with the `tray` step photo on the same page. `thin` shows tomato seedlings with true leaves, and `cells` shows filled cell trays.

A supply or equipment thumbnail does not claim to demonstrate every instruction alongside it. The water image shows a glass mister, not bottom watering. The cover image shows a clear vented dome over a seed tray, not a tested product. The sowing image shows a fiber mat; vegetable seed-starting instructions use the separate soil-filled cell-tray photo. The light image shows LED bars over ornamental potted plants and is not a claim about a tested product.

## Photo rules the build enforces

- One photo file per page view. When a photo repeats, the later copy is removed, and the build then fails if that leaves any list row, chart row, or step without a photo. Pick another photo instead of relying on removal. The three /gear/ supply panels are shown one at a time, so they may share photos with each other but not with anything else on the page.
- Related-guide cards try the photos in `RELATED_PHOTO_CHOICES` (scripts/build_site.py) in order and use the first one whose picture is not already on the page.
- The same picture may not appear twice on a page under different file names or URLs (a thumbnail and a WebP product copy, a crop, a resize). The build compares image content with ORB keypoints (`check_same_photo_content`); a pair that lines up with 60 or more matching points counts as the same photo. Results are kept in `scripts/photo-match-cache.json`, so OpenCV (`pip install opencv-python-headless numpy`) is only needed when a photo is added or changed. Separate frames from one session that look alike, such as Pannet's two sunflower tray photos, are listed in `SAME_PHOTO_SETS`. The /photo-credits/ page is exempt because it shows every photo.
- Every image (src and srcset) must load: the file exists and is not empty, or a remote URL returns 200 with an image content type.
- A photo credit with no image in the same row, card, or figure fails the build.
- Every content list item, table row, card, and step inside `<main>` needs a loaded photo (rows on the tray record, both seed charts, related guides, breadcrumbs, and On this page menus included). The only exemptions, in `PhotoListAudit`: the photo credits, privacy, and affiliate disclosure pages; lists inside `site-nav`, `sources`, or `footnotes`; and the site header and footer.
- Breadcrumbs and the On this page menu get small decorative thumbnails (`alt=""`, since the text names the target) picked by `add_nav_photos`. Each menu carries one 12px "Photos:" credit line naming every photographer, with the full credit on /photo-credits/.
- Every page under /guides/ and /tools/ must end with a Related guides block of at least 3 photo cards (`check_ends_with_related`). The email signup box may follow it. Source pages add one with `<!--RELATED:slug-->`.
- The seed charts on the calculator and the seed density guide use one crop photo per row (`CALC_CHART_PHOTOS`, `RATES_CHART_PHOTOS`, and the density chart list), each different from the calculator's package box photos.
- The small apartment guide's step 1 ("Clean the tray") now uses the `cells` photo, so the daily check's drainage row can use the draining-tray photo.
