Fortune Lab contains three working concepts. These are symbolic reflection experiences for entertainment, not validated predictions about personality, relationships, health or future events.

Astraline: a calculated traditional chart

Known birth times use the actual Renhuai123/ziwei-doushu engine, pinned to commit 39c0abd3b8dac609975dddfadd4d4e1d3a978deb. The browser bundle uses its MIT-licensed chart algorithm and required tables, with iztro and lunar-javascript dependencies.

The first version uses the entered local clock time, a split leap-month convention, and next-day charting for 23:00–23:59. It does not yet correct for longitude, birthplace timezone history or daylight saving. A chart near an hour boundary can change after those corrections. If the Life Palace is empty, its opposite palace's principal stars are identified as borrowed in the methodology. Featuring one star is an intentional simplification.

Unknown birth time produces a clearly labeled numerology reflection and no natal chart. All English archetypes, journal prompts and weekly plans are original editorial content. The large upstream interpretation dataset, lecture quotations and noncommercial corpus are not used in these demos. No external AI service generates the results.

Orbit Two: numbers and self-described preferences

The birth-date calculation adds all Gregorian date digits, then reduces while retaining 11, 22 or 33. The pairing uses those symbolic numbers along with the selected relationship type, focus and conversation styles. It does not calculate a Zi Wei compatibility chart or assign a likelihood of relationship success.

Starseed Rituals: a symbolic matching quiz

Answer-based scoring selects one of four fictionalized symbolic archetypes drawn from starseed culture. It is not a test of alien ancestry. Practical time and activity preferences shape the plan and product suggestions. Tea is described by ingredients, flavor and caffeine information, with no health or supernatural-effect claims.

Data and commercial status

The demos do not collect email addresses, create accounts or send quiz details to a server. Details remain in the tab and disappear on refresh. Copy and download actions share only the content you choose. No third-party analytics or advertising pixels are installed on these demo pages. Hosting providers and external merchants may process ordinary request information under their own policies.

Report prices are proposed tests. Full previews are free. There is no payment processor, recurring billing, merchant enrollment or active affiliate tracking. Product links open the named retailers using ordinary URLs. Symbolic product drawings are illustrative category art, not product photographs. Current prices, variants, ingredients and availability should be checked with the merchant.
