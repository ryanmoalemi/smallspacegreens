How this reading works

Orbit Two uses a digit-sum numerology method: add all the digits in each birth date, then reduce the sum to 1–9, retaining 11, 22 and 33 when encountered. We combine those symbolic themes with the relationship type, focus and conversation preferences you choose. The prompts and interpretations are original editorial content. This is an entertainment and reflection tool, not a scientific compatibility test or a prediction of your relationship.

We do not send or save the names, birthdays or reading on a server. Copying, downloading or printing is your choice. Product links open a merchant’s website, where its own privacy and purchase terms apply.
