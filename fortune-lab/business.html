Connection & conversation

Orbit Two

Audience hypothesis: couples, people exploring a new connection, and close friends who enjoy a shared experience. This concept has the clearest reason to share a result.

The customer journey

Two birthdays, relationship type and conversation preferences → symbolic pair reading → tailored conversation prompts → complete pair report.

No invented percentage or claim that dates predict relationship success.

How it earns

Test $19 once for a pair report and printable shared exercises. Test $29 only if a gift-ready version provides more value.

Couples cards and journals are the first product layer. Keen is a later referral candidate after approval and offer verification.

What to test first

Compare “your conversation rhythm” with “two birthdays, one connection.” Track shares that bring new visitors and purchases by relationship type.

The useful conversation output needs to feel personal enough to revisit.

Identity into ritual

Starseed Rituals

Audience hypothesis: people who enjoy starseed symbolism, oracle decks and intentional daily rituals. This applies your idea of an identity quiz that leads to a fitting product.

The customer journey

Four short choices → Pleiadian, Sirian, Arcturian or Andromedan archetype → seven-day ritual → a matching tea, journal or deck.

Archetypes are symbolic. Time and activity preferences determine practical product fit.

How it earns

Art of Tea affiliate sales plus journal/deck referrals, subject to program acceptance. Test a $9 original ritual workbook.

An owned tea or ritual kit could come later, after actual demand and supplier economics are established.

What to test first

Compare a starseed-identity hook with a practical “find your five-minute ritual” hook. Separate the audiences and track merchant revenue per entrant.

Small retail commissions make organic content and repeat visits especially important.

Would the traffic pay for itself?

This is a planning calculator, not a revenue forecast. The default scenario makes the difference between an owned report and retail commissions visible.

Payment illustration: 2.9% + $0.30 per paid transaction, using Stripe's published US domestic-card baseline. Eligibility, actual account fees and tax handling must be confirmed before launch.

We reserve the selected percentage of report sales for refunds while retaining modeled processing fees. Fractional purchases are expected-value estimates, not real transactions.

What the default numbers tell us: retail affiliate revenue alone leaves very little room for paid acquisition. An original paid report creates a larger possible contribution per visitor, but we still need real purchase, refund and advertising data before calling this profitable.

A concrete next step

Launch one offer, then learn.

Pick the experience and improve the paid deliverable

Choose the concept after trying all three. Expand and edit the report until it is useful enough to sell. Current previews demonstrate the complete journey and personalization.

Connect approved commerce

Confirm payment-provider eligibility; implement server-confirmed payment and report delivery, receipts and refunds. Apply to the chosen affiliate programs and replace product links with approved exact-product tracking links.

Instrument the funnel, with minimal data

Use existing hosting and a small server endpoint for fulfillment. Track concept, angle, quiz start, completion, report purchase and outbound product click. Never put names or birth details into URLs or advertising events. PostHog is a candidate, not connected.

Test two distinct messages per concept

Prepare separate persona cards and original image or short-video creatives. Start with organic distribution to inspect demand. Any paid test needs its own budget approval and should optimize collected revenue per visitor, not just quiz completions.
