Good advice has roots.

How we choose sources, compare products, and make the evidence clear.

Responsible editor: Ryan Moalemi. Updated November 10, 2026.

Growing advice you can check

Our growing guides draw on university extension services and public agencies. Sources include Penn State, Maryland, Utah State, Illinois, LSU AgCenter, and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Each guide links to the publications behind its instructions.

When sources use different tray sizes or methods, we explain that difference. A published starting rate is a planning aid, not a guaranteed result for every crop, seed lot, or growing environment.

Know what the evidence means

  • Published guidance. Advice attributed to a linked extension or agency publication.
  • Manufacturer specification. A product's listed size, contents, or compatibility. We record the exact variant and the date checked. This is not a measurement made by Small Space Greens.
  • Researched comparison. Our assessment of those published details against a practical need, with a limitation for each option.
  • Illustration. An original drawing or diagram that explains a method, measurement, or comparison. It is not evidence of a product's performance or a growing trial.
  • Licensed photo. Each photo credit links to its source, license, and crop details. These photographs do not show crops grown for this site.
  • User-entered number. A price or sowing rate entered into the calculator.
  • Firsthand test. Not used yet. Any future test will state its method, exact products, crop, conditions, dates, repeated trials, and limitations.

How we compare products

The setup guide compares kits, reusable trays, and reused containers, with a supply list for each. The cost comparison uses the prices you enter to total setup spending and show later-planting costs separately. These tools compare growing approaches; they do not rank specific products.

Some guides link to supplies. LetPot and True Leaf Market links use Awin. Other supply links use Amazon Associates. Each link is marked sponsored. A short commission sentence sits near the top of those pages, before the product links. Pages with Amazon links also say: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. See the affiliate disclosure. A brand's own store is not linked here until that program is approved.

Paid placements are not accepted. Any gifted product will be identified. A commission is never sufficient reason to recommend an item.

Current prices appear only when we can maintain an accurate source and comply with the relevant program's terms. Dated extension examples are labeled as historical, not current offers.

Updates and corrections

Ryan Moalemi is responsible for source checks and corrections. Email ryan@smallspacegreens.com with the page address and the detail that needs attention. We update the affected wording and retain the supporting source.
