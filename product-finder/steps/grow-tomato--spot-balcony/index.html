Fit, daily care & your first 3 steps

Measure first: Bag: 12 in diameter × 9.5 in high. Tiny Tim grows about 15–20 in above the mix. Leave room around the plant and protect the surface from drainage.

Timing: Tiny Tim variety estimate; allow seed-starting time too, usually 6–8 weeks before transplanting. Weather and light change timing.

Care: Check moisture daily; feed established plants according to the product label.

  1. Start a few seeds ¼ inch deep in moist mix and a warm, bright spot.
  2. Keep one strong plant for your grow bag. Move it outside gradually after frost risk has passed.
  3. Give it 6–8 hours of direct sun; check water daily and pick ripe fruit.
