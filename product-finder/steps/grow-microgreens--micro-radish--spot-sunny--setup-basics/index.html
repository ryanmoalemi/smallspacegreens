Fit, daily care & your first 3 steps

Measure first: Use one tray at a time. Leave open space above for shoots and access for watering. A dim spot also needs the separate standing light.

Timing: Broccoli and radish research estimates are 8–12 days under controlled conditions. Home window light can take longer.

Care: Check moisture daily; keep the medium moist and drain excess water.

  1. Clean the tray, moisten the medium and spread seed evenly; follow the kit or seed instructions.
  2. Uncover after germination and move into bright light. Water from below once roots establish.
  3. Cut shoots above the medium with clean scissors, then wash before eating. Re-sow for the next crop.
