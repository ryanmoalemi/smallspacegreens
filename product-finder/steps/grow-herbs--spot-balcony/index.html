Fit, daily care & your first 3 steps

Measure first: Each pot is 8 × 8 × 6.7 in. Allow extra width for saucers and leaves; two pots need more than a 16 × 8 in strip. Herbs may need larger pots as they mature.

Timing: Basil can give earlier picks; slower perennial herbs take longer. Follow each packet rather than one date for all twelve varieties.

Care: Keep varieties in separate pots so watering can match each herb.

  1. Choose two herbs you will actually cook with and follow their packet sowing depths.
  2. Give each its own starter pot and the light it needs; thin crowded seedlings.
  3. Pick lightly once established and move larger herbs into bigger pots.
