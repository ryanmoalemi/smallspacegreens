Fit, daily care & your first 3 steps

Measure first: Each pot is 8 × 8 × 6.7 in. Allow extra width for saucers and leaves; two pots need more than a 16 × 8 in strip. Herbs may need larger pots as they mature.

Timing: Baby leaves first; a fuller Buttercrunch head takes longer. Window light and heat can delay or reduce harvest.

Care: Keep mix evenly moist and protect leaves from excessive heat.

  1. Sow a few seeds near the surface, following the packet depth.
  2. Thin to one lettuce plant per pot; keep the mix moist and the plants well lit.
  3. Pick outer leaves once large enough, leaving at least half the plant growing.
