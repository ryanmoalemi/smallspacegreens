Fit, daily care & your first 3 steps

Measure first: Each pot is 8 × 8 × 6.7 in. Allow extra width for saucers and leaves; two pots need more than a 16 × 8 in strip. Herbs may need larger pots as they mature.

Timing: From seed, once plants are established. Warmth, light and variety affect timing.

Care: Check moisture and pinch growing tips to encourage branching.

  1. Sow basil ¼ inch deep in moist potting mix.
  2. Thin to one strong plant per starter pot and keep it warm and well lit.
  3. Pinch above a pair of leaves once established; upsize the pot as the plant grows.
