How product links work

A clear explanation of recommendations and commissions.

Updated November 10, 2026.

We earn a commission from purchases through this page. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Current status: Some guides link to LetPot countertop gardens and to True Leaf Market seed and kit collections through Awin, and to other supplies through Amazon Associates. The gift guide, the microgreen seeds guide, and the setup guide do too. The setup guide names specific supplies and still compares a kit, reusable trays, and a container you already own.

LetPot links through Awin

The program link goes to LetPot. Individual guides link to specific models. Awin provides the tracking, and the purchase happens on LetPot's site. Confirm the model, current price, delivery, returns, and whether it is in stock there. A link does not mean this site has grown that garden. There is no coupon code.

True Leaf Market links through Awin

The program link goes to True Leaf Market. Individual guides link to seed and kit collections. Awin provides the tracking, and the purchase happens on True Leaf Market's site. Confirm the seed or kit, current price, delivery, returns, and whether it is in stock there. A link does not mean this site has grown that seed. There is no coupon code.

Amazon Associates links

Some guides, the setup page, the gift guide, and the seed calculator link to a specific product. Supply cards use a photo, the product name, one short reason, and a button that says View on Amazon, See at True Leaf, or See at LetPot. Those photos are licensed stand-ins, not Amazon's photos. Microgreens seed links go to True Leaf Market, and LetPot gardens go to LetPot, instead of Amazon. Confirm the variant, current price, delivery, returns, and whether it is in stock on the seller's page. A link does not mean this site has used that product. Prices are not copied here. There is no coupon code.

Our recommendations

Growing steps come from published extension sources. A LetPot note repeats what LetPot's own page says. A True Leaf Market note describes the collection, not a growing result. Read the editorial method for the details.

A company cannot pay for a more favorable recommendation. You can use every guide and the calculator without clicking a product link.

Cookies and questions

LetPot, True Leaf Market, Awin, and Amazon have their own privacy and cookie policies. An affiliate link can use tracking to attribute a purchase. Our privacy policy explains the services this site uses.
