Gear for a small tray

Categories only. No brand names, prices, or store links until an affiliate program is approved.

  • Tray pair

    One tray with holes and one without, so you can water from below. A clean food container with holes, set in a solid tray, is a valid start. No specific tray is listed yet.

  • Mix or mat

    Soilless mix or coir, about 1 to 1.5 inches if you use mix. Skip garden soil. No mix brand is listed yet.

  • Lamp and timer

    Add light when a window leaves stems pale or leaning. No fixture is named until one is approved and checked.

  • Mister

    For the first days, so seed stays put. Switch to bottom watering after the roots hold.

  • Scissors

    Cut above the mix and leave the roots. Any clean, sharp scissors will do.

  • Fan

    Moving air after you uncover the tray. Useful in a still room. It does not make a moldy crop safe to eat.
