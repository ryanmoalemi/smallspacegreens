Gear and comparisons

Categories only. No product names, prices, or affiliate links until a specific item is checked.

  • Tray pair

    One tray with holes, one without. Inner floor size has not been measured for any model sold here, because nothing is sold here yet.

  • Mix or mat

    Soilless mix or coir, about 1 to 1.5 inches if you use mix. Garden soil is the thing to skip.

  • Lamp and timer

    Add light when a window leaves stems pale or leaning. No fixture is named until clearance and coverage are checked.

  • Mister

    For the first days, so seed stays put. Bottom water after roots hold.

  • Scissors

    Cut above the mix. Leave the roots.

  • Fan

    Moving air after uncovering. Useful in a still apartment. Not a cure for a moldy crop.
